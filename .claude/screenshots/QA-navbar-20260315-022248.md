### Technical Analysis

The Shopify nav bar closely matches the Lovable reference design for all key structural and visual properties:

1. **Layout structure**: Both sites use a single-row horizontal flex layout with logo on the far left, a full-width search bar in the center, and the vehicle selector + cart icon on the far right. This matches the spec requirement.

2. **Vehicle selector placement**: The "SELECT YOUR VEHICLE" orange button is correctly positioned inside the nav bar on the right side, NOT in a separate row below. This satisfies the explicit spec requirement. On Shopify, it is rendered within the `header-actions` area of the Horizon theme header.

3. **Color scheme**: Both use a dark background with white text and orange accent for the vehicle selector button. The exact hex values differ slightly (#111 vs #1a1a1a) but the visual appearance is indistinguishable at a glance.

4. **Search bar**: Both sites feature a prominent full-width search input spanning the space between the logo and the right-side actions. The Shopify implementation uses the Horizon theme's predictive search component.

5. **The only visible differences are the intentional ones**: font family (Geist Mono vs Barlow Condensed) and the Shopify announcement bar above the nav. Both are excluded from evaluation per the spec.

### Overall: PASS

All nav bar properties match between Lovable reference and Shopify implementation. The vehicle selector is correctly positioned inside the nav bar row on the right side. No fixes needed.
